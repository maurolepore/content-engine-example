About Me and Briix

Who I am
Max Johnson (goes by MJ). Based in Manchester, UK. Founder of Briix, an AI consultancy and agency operating under BRIIX LTD. Built it from scratch after leaving a career in construction.

The content across every platform documents that build in real time — not a highlight reel. That includes the early near-quitting moments, the slow client acquisition, and the pivots, not just the wins. The working concept behind a chunk of that content is "what happens when you don't quit" — a documentary-style account of actually building something, rather than a polished founder narrative.

What Briix does
Practical AI implementation for small and medium businesses — not theory, not strategy decks.

- AI audits — going into a business and identifying where AI automation applies, rather than where it's trendy to apply it
- Automation builds — the actual implementation: workflows, integrations
- Workshops — in-person and online sessions teaching business owners AI fundamentals
- Integration services — connecting the tools a business already uses so automation actually works inside their existing operations

The pitch, in plain terms: a business tells you what they actually need, you either implement it yourself or pay someone to do it — rather than spending months filtering through noise hoping something "might work."

The other things I run
- AI Inner Circle — a paid community focused on AI implementation at an advanced level, not a course full of theory.
- AI Agency Builder Program — a high-ticket program teaching founders to build their own AI agencies, built from having actually done it.
- CartRiser — a separate, AI-powered CRO (conversion rate optimisation) tool for UK DTC food, beverage, and supplement brands, on a performance-only pricing model (a share of the uplift, not a flat fee) — a deliberately different commercial model from Briix's agency work.

Team
Small team. Jamie and Adrian work on operations and delivery. Additional writers work from the same voice rules for platform-specific content, but everything is reviewed against this voice before it goes out — nothing ships that reads like a content agency wrote it.

Positioning
Practitioner, not guru. Everything grounded in what's actually been built and tested — real clients, real automations, real numbers — not hype about what AI "could" do. The audience should come away thinking "this person actually does this," not "this person sells courses about doing this."

On LinkedIn specifically, the north-star reference points are founders who write raw, specific, unpolished content rather than performed vulnerability or generic thought leadership — documenting reality rather than performing struggle for engagement, and taking a genuine, plainly-stated position rather than hedging.

Where I show up
Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn as @maxjohnson. Daily short-form content about AI tools, automation, and entrepreneurship on Instagram and TikTok. LinkedIn is a slower, more deliberate channel aimed at business owners already in a decision-making mindset — case studies and direct opinion rather than the same short-form content.
