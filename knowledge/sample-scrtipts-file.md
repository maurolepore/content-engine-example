Sample Scripts

Three examples across three platforms. Scripts 1 and 2 are real, pulled from past sessions. Script 3 is newly written for this file, built directly from the established LinkedIn case-study framework and voice rules — not a literal past post, but structured exactly the way real ones are.

Script 1 — TikTok, explainer, ~28 seconds
Topic: "Everything you need to know about OpenClaw"

[HOOK — 0-3s] "Here's everything you need to know about OpenClaw"
[WHAT IT IS — 3-10s] "It's an AI assistant that lives on your computer and actually does things — manages your emails, books your flights, runs your calendar — all through WhatsApp or Telegram like texting a coworker."
[MAC MINI QUESTION — 10-18s] "Everyone's asking: do you need a Mac Mini? The short answer is no — it runs on any computer. But people are getting Mac Minis because they want a dedicated 24/7 machine where the AI can work in the background without touching their main computer."
[SECURITY — 18-26s] "On security: OpenClaw just partnered with VirusTotal to scan all plugins for malware, but because it runs locally and has full access to your system, you need to be careful what you give it permission to do."
[CLOSER — 26-28s] "It's open source, free to use — you just pay for the AI tokens."

Why it works: hook states exactly what the viewer gets, no throat-clearing. Every section answers a real question people were actually asking, not a manufactured one.

Script 2 — Instagram Reels, ad-style
Core message: businesses are falling behind without AI

[HOOK — straight to camera, deadpan] "Your competitor just automated half their workflow. You're still copy-pasting into spreadsheets."
[BODY] "Here's what's actually happening right now. Businesses using AI are cutting admin time by 30, 40, sometimes 60 percent. They're responding to leads faster, running leaner teams, and reinvesting that time into growth. The ones not doing it aren't just missing out — they're actively falling behind. And the gap is widening every single month. This isn't about replacing your team. It's about making sure you're not the business that gets replaced."
[CTA] "If you don't know where AI fits in your business, that's the problem. Start there."

Why it works: specific numbers (30/40/60%) instead of a vague "more efficient," and the CTA doesn't ask for anything overtly salesy.

Script 3 — LinkedIn, case study post
Built from the real case-study framework: name the problem, the situation, the work, the outcome, the pull, then the CTA.

Hook (lines 1-2): "A client was manually following up with every single lead by hand. It was taking her three hours a day."
The situation: She runs a small consultancy, no dedicated ops person, every lead handled personally because "it felt too important to automate." Three hours a day gone before any actual client work started.
The work: We built a single automation in n8n that watches the inbox, drafts a personalised follow-up in her voice, and logs it — she reviews and sends in under a minute per lead instead of writing each one from scratch.
The outcome: Same three hours a day now goes into actual client delivery. She's taken on two more clients since, without hiring.
The pull: Most business owners we talk to have some version of this sitting in their operations — something that feels too important to automate, until it's costing them a full day a week.
CTA: "If this sounds familiar, happy to have a look at what's going on in your business. Details in the comments." (link goes in the first comment, never the post body)

Why it works: the number (three hours a day — two new clients) is concrete and believable rather than an inflated marketing claim, and the frame is the client's story, not a pitch.
